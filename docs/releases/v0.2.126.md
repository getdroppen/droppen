## Droppen App for macOS

`Droppen-0.2.126.dmg` — the desktop app (Apple silicon and Intel), signed with Developer ID and notarised by Apple. It needs macOS 13 or later. Open the DMG, drag Droppen to Applications, open it.

Droppen 0.2.125 updates itself to this release: it downloads it in the background and installs it the next time you quit, or at once with *Restart to Update*. Version 0.2.124 and older do not update themselves: download this DMG once and replace the app in Applications.

## macOS 版 Droppen App

`Droppen-0.2.126.dmg` 是桌面 App（Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片的 Mac 都能用），使用 Developer ID 签名并经过 Apple 公证，需要 macOS 13 或更高版本。打开 DMG，把 Droppen 拖到「应用程序」，然后打开它。

0.2.125 会自动更新到这个版本：在后台下载，并在你下次退出时安装；也可以用「重启以更新」立即安装。0.2.124 及更早的版本不会自动更新：请手动下载一次这个 DMG，替换「应用程序」里的 Droppen。

## What's new in v0.2.126

### Devices that run AI (the server `droppen`, and the one inside the Droppen App)

- **Relays now work behind a fake-ip proxy.** With a proxy in fake-ip mode (Clash, mihomo or Stash in fake-ip mode, Surge's enhanced mode), every name resolves to an address in `198.18.0.0/15` and the proxy makes the real connection. Droppen refused those addresses, so such a computer never joined a relay (its log repeated "it resolves to 198.18.0.92, which this agent does not dial") and your browsers and phones could reach it only through Droppen Hub. It now connects to its relays through the proxy. The exception is narrow: only for a relay's name whose addresses are all in that range, and only over an encrypted connection that checks the relay's own name. A relay given as such an address, or a name that also resolves to anything else, is still refused. If you kept the relays' names out of fake-ip as a workaround, you no longer need to.

### Web app (droppen.app and the page on your device)

- **droppen.app/security**, a new page: how Droppen's end-to-end encryption works, for anyone who wants to check it. Who holds which key, how your devices agree on who belongs to your account, what Droppen Hub and the relays can and cannot see, and what each of them could still do. In English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Japanese, linked from the footer of the public pages.
- **Security: the record of your device list is kept safely across tabs.** Each browser remembers the newest device list it has verified for your account, so that it cannot be shown an older or a different one. That record now lives in the browser's database and is checked and updated in one step, so tabs open at the same time can no longer overwrite each other's record. A record that cannot be read for a moment is tried again: it is never taken for a different list, or for no record at all.
- The footer of the public pages links the third-party licences of the web app, now served beside the page (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`).

### Not enabled yet

- Groundwork for renewing a device's identity key by itself, instead of asking you to enroll the device again, is in the web app, the server and the protocol. It stays switched off: nothing changes for now.

## v0.2.126 更新内容

### 可运行 AI 的设备（服务端 `droppen`，以及 Droppen App 内置的服务）

- **在 fake-ip 代理下也能连上中继了。** 代理开着 fake-ip 模式时（Clash、mihomo 或 Stash 的 fake-ip 模式，Surge 的增强模式），所有域名都会解析到 `198.18.0.0/15` 里的地址，再由代理建立真正的连接。Droppen 以前拒绝这些地址，所以这样的电脑一直连不上任何中继（日志反复出现 "it resolves to 198.18.0.92, which this agent does not dial"），你的浏览器和手机只能经由 Droppen Hub 访问它。现在它会通过代理连接中继。这个例外很窄：只针对解析结果全部落在这个范围内的中继域名，而且只走会校验中继自身域名的加密连接。直接写成这类地址的中继，或者同时还解析到其它地址的域名，仍然会被拒绝。如果你之前把中继的域名排除在 fake-ip 之外作为临时办法，现在不需要了。

### 网页（droppen.app 和设备上的本地页面）

- **droppen.app/security** 是新页面：讲 Droppen 的端到端加密是怎么做的，写给想自己核实的人。哪把钥匙在谁手里，你的设备怎样就「谁属于这个账户」达成一致，Droppen Hub 和中继能看到什么、看不到什么，以及它们仍然可能做什么。提供英文、简体中文、繁体中文和日文版本，公开页面的页脚有链接。
- **安全：设备列表的记录在多个标签页之间也能可靠保存。** 每个浏览器都会记住它为你的账户验证过的最新设备列表，这样别人就无法给它看一份更旧或不同的列表。这份记录现在保存在浏览器的数据库里，检查和更新一步完成，同时打开的标签页不会再互相覆盖记录。记录一时读不出来时会重试：既不会被当成一份不同的列表，也不会被当成没有记录。
- 公开页面的页脚链接了网页所用第三方软件的许可证，现在和页面一起提供（`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`）。

### 尚未启用

- 设备身份密钥自动续期（不再要求你重新加入设备）的基础工作已经进入网页、服务端和协议，但仍处于关闭状态：目前不会有任何变化。
