## Droppen App for macOS

`Droppen-0.2.132.dmg` — the desktop app (Apple silicon and Intel), signed with Developer ID and notarised by Apple. It needs macOS 13 or later. Open the DMG, drag Droppen to Applications, open it.

Droppen 0.2.127 to 0.2.131 update themselves to this release: they download it in the background and install it the next time you quit, or at once with *Restart to Update*. Droppen 0.2.125 and 0.2.126 update themselves in two steps: first to 0.2.127, which https://github.com/getdroppen/releases (where they look for updates, now archived) still offers, then from 0.2.127 to this release. Version 0.2.124 and older do not update themselves: download this DMG once and replace the app in Applications.

## macOS 版 Droppen App

`Droppen-0.2.132.dmg` 是桌面 App（Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片的 Mac 都能用），使用 Developer ID 签名并经过 Apple 公证，需要 macOS 13 或更高版本。打开 DMG，把 Droppen 拖到「应用程序」，然后打开它。

0.2.127 至 0.2.131 会自动更新到这个版本：在后台下载，并在你下次退出时安装；也可以用「重启以更新」立即安装。0.2.125 和 0.2.126 会分两步自动更新：先更新到 0.2.127（它们检查更新的 https://github.com/getdroppen/releases 已归档，但仍提供 0.2.127），再从 0.2.127 更新到这个版本。0.2.124 及更早的版本不会自动更新：请手动下载一次这个 DMG，替换「应用程序」里的 Droppen。

## What's new in v0.2.132

### Connecting the device your AI runs on

- **My devices shows how to connect it.** While your account has no device that runs AI, my devices shows a card, *Connect the device your AI runs on*, made for the device the page is open on, where there used to be one line of text. It starts with what that device needs: Claude Code or Codex, installed and signed in.
  - On a Mac: *Download Droppen for Mac*, with its version and size, then three steps: install it, open it, and Sign in to Droppen in the App.
  - On Linux, a Chromebook included (turn Linux on first): the install command, with *copy command*, and a one-time code for a device with no browser, such as a server.
  - On Windows, which has no app yet: your AI needs a Mac or a Linux device with Droppen on it. WSL works too, with systemd turned on; the card links Microsoft's how-to.
  - On a phone or tablet: the download page's address, to *share link* or *copy link* to the device your AI runs on.
  - When a device that runs AI joins, the card gives way to a short confirmation, with *open*. Until then, this device's own settings (direct connections, relays, the standby relay) and key rotation are folded under *more settings*, below the list.
- **droppen.app says that the App signs itself in, and where.** The download page's App tab now ends with "Click Sign in to Droppen in the app", and says where it is: the App opens a short setup guide, four steps; in its step 02 (Where to use it), choose "On my phone and other devices too", then Sign in to Droppen (later: Settings → Access → Droppen). It used to say that signing in from the App was coming in an update. The add-a-device guide now starts a Mac with the App's download.
- **The one-time code shows the time it has left.** In the add-a-device guide, the one-time code, for a Linux server or any device with no browser, shows how long it has left ("The code expires in 9:42.") and asks you to keep the page open, since the page adds the device once the command runs there. When it runs out, the command gives way to "This code has expired." and *new code*. A device that used the code in time is still added.
- *Bring it over* is offered on the sign-in tab only: a new account has nothing to bring over.
- `install.sh`'s summary names the button in English, "Sign in to Droppen", where it named the Chinese one.

### Your devices

- **droppen.app shows each device's system and Droppen version.** A device that runs AI shows its system as it reports it, such as `macOS 15.3.1 aarch64` or `ubuntu 24.04 x86_64`, and the version of Droppen it runs, in my devices. droppen.app used to show only `macOS`. A device with an older Droppen shows what it showed before.
- **A veto shows what it removes before it removes anything.** When this browser holds back a device's early self-activation, *remove it and what it brought in* now opens a confirmation that lists what goes: the held device, then each device brought in with it, who brought it in and whether it runs AI, and whether the account's recovery words stop working.
- A separate choice, off by default, also removes the devices your other devices added while this browser held the activation back, and what those added. Leave it off if you added them yourself.
- The veto holds to what you confirmed. If the account's device list moves on after you looked, or another activation is held back meanwhile, it signs nothing and asks you to look again; and a hold that ends while the veto is under way no longer changes what it removes.

### Downloads and privacy

- **Downloads from droppen.app are counted, and nothing is kept about who downloads.** Where droppen.app names its own download link for a file (`/dl/<platform>`), the download buttons go through it: it adds one to that day's count for that download, then sends you on to the file on GitHub. Otherwise they link the file on GitHub, as before. No IP address, browser data or cookie is kept: the address and the network a download came from stay in memory for an hour at most, only to keep one source from inflating the counts. droppen.app/privacy has a new section, "If you download Droppen", that says so, and droppen.app's `robots.txt` asks crawlers to stay out of `/dl/`.
- **Rate limits forget every address within a day.** A relay counts requests per address only to limit them. It now forgets an address once its limits have filled up again, and a day after its last request at the latest; a quiet relay used to keep one until its table filled up or it restarted. Droppen Hub follows the same rule for its own counts. The privacy notice now says that requests are counted per address and per network, in memory, for a day at most. If you run a relay, build its image again from v0.2.132 (README, "Running a relay").

## v0.2.132 更新内容

### 连接跑 AI 的那台设备

- **「我的设备」会告诉你怎么连接它。** 账户里还没有可运行 AI 的设备时，「我的设备」会显示一张卡片「连接跑 AI 的那台设备」，按打开页面的这台设备来写，取代以前的一行文字。卡片先说明那台设备需要什么：装好并登录了 Claude Code 或 Codex。
  - 在 Mac 上：「下载 Mac 版 Droppen」，标着版本和大小，然后是三步：安装、打开，在 App 里「登录 Droppen」。
  - 在 Linux 上（包括 Chromebook，先打开 Linux）：安装命令和「复制命令」，以及给没有浏览器的设备（比如服务器）用的注册码。
  - 在还没有 App 的 Windows 上：你的 AI 需要一台装了 Droppen 的 Mac 或 Linux 设备。WSL 也可以，但要打开 systemd；卡片链接到微软的说明。
  - 在手机或平板上：下载页的地址，可以「分享链接」或「复制链接」到跑 AI 的那台设备。
  - 有可运行 AI 的设备加入后，卡片会换成一条简短的确认，并带「打开」。在此之前，这台设备自己的设置（直连、中继、备用中继）和钥匙轮换都收在列表下方的「更多设置」里。
- **droppen.app 写明 App 可以自己登录，以及在哪里登录。** 下载页的 App 一栏现在最后一步是「在 App 里点“登录 Droppen”」，并写明它在哪里：App 会打开一个四步的设置向导；在向导第 02 步「在哪里用」选「在手机和其它设备上也能用」，再点「登录 Droppen」（之后也可以在「设置 → 访问 → Droppen」里找到）。以前它写的是从 App 登录要等以后的更新。添加设备的向导现在在 Mac 上先给出 App 的下载。
- **注册码会显示剩余时间。** 在添加设备的向导里，给 Linux 服务器或任何没有浏览器的设备用的注册码，现在会显示还剩多久（「注册码有效期还剩 9:42。」），并提醒你保持页面打开：命令在那台设备上运行后，由这个页面完成添加。过期后，命令会换成「这个注册码已过期。」和「换个新注册码」。在有效期内用了注册码的设备，仍会被添加。
- 「迁移过来」只在登录一栏里提供：新账户没有什么可迁移的。
- `install.sh` 的英文总结里，现在写的是英文按钮名「Sign in to Droppen」，而不是中文的。

### 你的设备

- **droppen.app 显示每台设备的系统和 Droppen 版本。** 可运行 AI 的设备，在「我的设备」里会显示它自己报告的系统，比如 `macOS 15.3.1 aarch64` 或 `ubuntu 24.04 x86_64`，以及它运行的 Droppen 版本。以前 droppen.app 只显示 `macOS`。运行旧版 Droppen 的设备，显示的内容和以前一样。
- **否决之前，先列出会移除什么。** 当这个浏览器暂缓了某台设备过早的自行激活时，「移除它及它带进来的一切」现在会先打开一个确认，列出会移除的设备：被暂缓的设备，然后是随它带进来的每台设备，谁把它带进来，它是否可运行 AI，以及账户的恢复词是否会失效。
- 另有一个单独的选项，默认关闭：同时移除你的其他设备在本浏览器暂缓期间添加的设备，以及这些设备再添加的设备。如果那些设备是你自己添加的，请保持关闭。
- 否决会严格按你确认的内容执行：如果在你查看之后账户的设备列表有了新变化，或期间又有另一次激活被暂缓，它不会签署任何内容，并请你重新查看；否决进行中暂缓恰好结束，也不会再改变它移除的内容。

### 下载与隐私

- **从 droppen.app 下载会被计数，但不保留任何关于下载者的信息。** 当 droppen.app 为某个文件提供自己的下载链接（`/dl/<平台>`）时，下载按钮会经过它：它把那一天这个下载的计数加一，再把你转到 GitHub 上的文件；否则按钮和以前一样直接链接 GitHub 上的文件。不保留 IP 地址、浏览器信息或 Cookie：下载来自的地址和网络最多在内存里保留一小时，只用来防止同一来源刷高计数。droppen.app/privacy 新增一节「如果你下载了 Droppen」说明这一点；droppen.app 的 `robots.txt` 请爬虫不要访问 `/dl/`。
- **限流计数在一天内忘掉每个地址。** 中继按地址计数请求，只是为了限流。现在，一个地址的额度恢复满之后它就会忘掉这个地址，最迟在最后一次请求一天之后；以前，请求很少的中继会一直记着，直到表满了或中继重启。Droppen Hub 自己的计数也遵循同样的规则。隐私说明现在写明：请求按地址和按网络在内存中计数，最多保留一天。如果你自己运行中继，请用 v0.2.132 重新构建它的镜像（见 README 的「Running a relay」）。
